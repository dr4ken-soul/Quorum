# Quorum - Marketing Notes

## Positioning

Quorum is the pre-payment pause button for group chats. It turns a suspicious request into a case, gathers the receipts, and gives the group a reasoned next step before anyone transfers money.

## Short description

Put the payment request on trial before you pay.

## Demo opening

`Can you send the deposit now?`

`Objection. Opening the case.`

## Core message

The product is useful because it works inside the conversation where the pressure happens. It is memorable because the conversation becomes a court without becoming a game.

## Three proof points

1. One forwarded message starts the review.
2. Every conclusion points to an exhibit or names what is missing.
3. The agent never makes the final payment decision for the user.

## Demo structure

1. Show the urgent request.
2. Forward it to the Photon agent.
3. Show extraction of amount, account, event, and deadline.
4. Show one real contradiction from an evidence adapter.
5. Let a participant object in the group chat.
6. Show the `PAUSE` verdict and case receipt.
7. End with the limitation: verify independently before sending money.

## Social post draft

We put a payment request on trial inside iMessage.

Quorum collects the receipts, lets the group object, and returns PAY, PAUSE, or WALK AWAY before the transfer.

Built on Photon.

## Tone

- Dry rather than loud
- Specific rather than grand
- Funny during the hearing
- Serious at the ruling
- Never sensationalise a real scam victim

## Avoid

- Scam detector
- Guaranteed protection
- AI judge that knows the truth
- Automatic payment blocking
- Fear-based statistics without a source
- Unapproved Photon or Apple marks
