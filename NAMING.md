# Naming Direction

## Current status

The previous working name, ProofBeforePay, is a product description rather than a brand name. It should remain an internal concept label, not the public product name.

## Recommended name

### Quorum

Quorum describes the minimum agreement needed for a group decision. It fits the product because the agent gathers evidence and trusted responses before returning a ruling.

The name is single-word, pronounceable, and serious enough for a trust product while still supporting the Group Chat Court presentation.

## Alternatives

| Name | Meaning | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| Docket | A record of a case | Strong court metaphor, more administrative |
| Vouch | Trusted confirmation | Clear and friendly, less distinctive |
| Writ | A written legal instrument | Elegant and unusual, less immediately clear |
| Bailiff | Court officer who keeps order | Very memorable, less serious as a product brand |

## Naming rules

- Use one word or a short invented word
- Avoid names that promise certainty or protection
- Avoid compound names unless they are unusually natural
- Do not imply a bank, insurer, legal service, or guarantee
- Confirm domain, social handle, trademark, and app-store availability before launch

## Decision required

The documents use Quorum as a provisional name. Replace it everywhere after the final single-word name is approved. The interface must use a text-only wordmark until then.
